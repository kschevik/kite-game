Knut's Kite Game

  1. Drag to steer the kite. Swoop it through the power zone to build speed. Don't park it.
  2. Hit POP (Space) on the wave lip to jump. Perfect timing = big air.
  3. In the air: LOOP (L), SPIN (S), hold GRAB (G), BOARD OFF (B, press again to catch), PASS (H). Land clean!
